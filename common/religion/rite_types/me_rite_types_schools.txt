# Schools & Branches: the lesser madhhabs and branches (from the Madhabs submod).
# New Rites only. Nothing here redefines another Rite or faith.
#
# Tenets: every tenet below is Permitted, or part of the main Rite's core, for the faith that holds the Rite
# (see history/faiths/00_islam.txt), so the new Rites are accepted Rites of their faith and not heresies.
# Two exceptions are only Known for the Sunni faith: Unrelenting Faith (Zahiri) and, since 1.10.0,
# Pursuit of Knowledge (Jariri). Each adds a little divergence; neither makes the Rite a heresy.
# No two Sunni Rites share the same three tenets (see also me_rite_types_sunni_madhhabs.txt).
# Doctrines follow the pattern of the existing Rites of the same faith.
#
#   Sunni faith          zahiri, awzai, jariri, thawri, laythi
#   Zaydi faith          nasiri, mutarrifi
#   Kharijite faith      hamzite
#   Ibadi faith          rustaqi, khalafi
#   Isma'ili faith       satpanth
#   Ghulat faith         mushashai
#   Alevi faith          hurufi
