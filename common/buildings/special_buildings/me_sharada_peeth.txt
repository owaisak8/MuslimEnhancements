# Sharada Peeth (b_kaghan, province 7949): the temple of Sharada (Sarasvati) on the Kishanganga (Neelum),
# at the mouth of the Madhumati stream. A map fit on 12 anchors puts Sharda village inside Kaghan, which
# covers the Neelum valley. Sources: Nilamata Purana (earliest mention); Kalhana, Rajatarangini (Gauda
# men in the guise of pilgrims under Lalitaditya; Jayasimha's war at Sirahsila by Sharada, 1140s);
# al-Biruni, Kitab al-Hind (wooden image, much venerated and visited); Prabhavakacarita (Hemachandra's
# grammars from the goddess's library in Kashmir); Jonaraja (Zayn al-Abidin's visit, 1422).
# Whether it held a real school is debated (no school ruins survive); vanilla treats Somapura the same way.
# Tier 1 shrine, 2 stone temple (867 start), 3 seat of learning (1066/1178), 4 the Sarvajna Pitha legend.
