# ME: vanilla's two-tier lahur_bazaar (00_special_buildings.txt, placed at game start in game_start.txt) carried over
# with slightly stronger tiers 1 and 2, plus two new tiers. The file sorts after vanilla's so these copies win.
# Tiers 3 and 4 follow vanilla's commerce innovations (Merchant Law, then the late medieval Currency) and need
# a level 3/4 holding district, like ME's other four-tier buildings. Horses join cotton and salt as needs at
# tier 3: the Punjab was the road by which horses from beyond the passes reached Hindustan.
